Thornvale Skirmish

Marshal Wren holds the camp at the edge of Thornvale. Take her three quests, level up your mage, and bring down the ogre Old Gnash.

Controls
Move
WASD or arrow keys, or click/tap the ground
Target
Click/tap an enemy. Tab cycles nearby enemies.
Spells
1–5 or click the action bar
Talk
Click the marshal, or press F when close
Tips

Moving cancels a cast. Frost Nova freezes nearby enemies so you can step back and cast Fireball. Drink between fights. When Old Gnash raises his club, get out of the red circle.
